What Expenses are Covered?

Your GradBridge loan can be used to cover school-certified cost of attendance expenses.

```

✅ Typically Includes

  • ✓ Tuition and academic fees
  • ✓ On-campus or off-campus housing
  • ✓ Rent, utilities, and groceries
  • ✓ Meal plans or dining expenses
  • ✓ Books, supplies, and lab materials
  • ✓ A computer, software, or academic equipment
  • ✓ Transportation and commuting costs

  • ✗ Clothing, electronics unrelated to school
```

FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions.

```
Who is eligible for a GradBridge Student Loan?⌄
You are eligible if you are a U.S. Citizen or permanent resident, at least the age of majority in your state, and an undergraduate (junior or greater) or graduate student enrolled in an eligible U.S. school seeking a bachelor's or graduate/post-graduate degree.
Do I need a credit history to get a private student loan?⌄
Yes, there are credit history requirements. Since most students have limited credit history, you will likely need a cosigner who has established credit history and income.
What is a cosigner and do I need one?⌄
A cosigner can be a parent, grandparent, aunt or uncle, guardian, or mentor with established credit history and income. A cosigner agrees to take responsibility for repayment if you can't make payments. All undergraduate students will need a cosigner. For graduate students, applying with a cosigner can increase approval chances and may result in a lower interest rate.
How quickly can I get a decision?⌄
The GradBridge application generally takes less than 15 minutes to complete. After submitting, you'll receive a credit decision immediately. Once approved, the entire process — from application to funds at your school — can take as few as 10 business days.
Will loan funds be sent to me or to my school?⌄
All loans are disbursed directly to the school. The funds may be divided into multiple disbursements, usually one per semester. Your school will refund any excess money to you following their refund procedures.
```
