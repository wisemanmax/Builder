Eligibility Requirements

Simple requirements to get approved for your student loan

Student Requirements

  • • Enrolled at an eligible school
  • • U.S. citizen or permanent resident
  • • At least 18 years old
  • • Minimum 2.0 GPA
  • • Making satisfactory academic progress

Credit Requirements

  • • Minimum credit score of 650
  • • 2+ years of credit history
  • • Stable income or co-signer
  • • Debt-to-income ratio under 50%
  • • No recent bankruptcies

Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common questions about student loans

What is the minimum credit score required?

▼

The minimum credit score is 650 for most loan products. However, having a co-signer with excellent credit can help you qualify even with a lower score.

How long does the application process take?

▼

Most applications are completed in 5-10 minutes, and you'll receive an instant decision. Once approved, funds are typically sent to your school within 1-2 business days.

Can I pay off my loan early without penalties?

▼

Yes! All GradBridge loans can be paid off early without any prepayment penalties. You'll save money on interest by paying off your loan ahead of schedule.

What schools are eligible for loans?

▼

We work with most Title IV eligible schools in the United States. This includes public and private universities, community colleges, and trade schools.
