About GradBridge

Empowering the next generation of graduate students

Our Mission

GradBridge was founded with a simple mission: to democratize access to graduate education by providing students with the tools, resources, and connections they need to succeed. We believe that every student deserves the opportunity to pursue their academic dreams, regardless of their background or circumstances.

Our Story

Founded in 2023 by a team of graduate students and education professionals, GradBridge emerged from the recognition that the graduate school application process is often overwhelming and inequitable. Our founders experienced firsthand the challenges of navigating applications, finding mentors, and accessing resources.
